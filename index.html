The process, in five phases

  1. AnalyzeSurvey, interviews, and observation confirmed a skill gap that training could close.
  2. DesignFive measurable objectives, each with two aligned assessment items.
  3. DevelopA self-paced course with a job aid, demonstrations, and practice with feedback.
  4. ImplementTimed to release just before sprint planning, so learners use it right away.
  5. EvaluateSME review, one-to-one, small-group, and field trials before scaling.
